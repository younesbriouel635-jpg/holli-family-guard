# HOLLI Family Wealth

Act as an Elite UI/UX & Frontend Developer. Build a gorgeous, highly-addictive, multi-lingual family financial ecosystem app named "HOLLI" (Financial Guardrail & Wealth-Building App), optimized strictly for Mobile screens (iOS/Android layout). The app operates as a synchronized family network linking Parents and Kids (<18) accounts in real-time, featuring custom goal creation and predictive AI financial feasibility solutions.

Design Aesthetic & Brand Identity:

- Brand Name: HOLLI (Ultra-premium typography logo).

- Style: Strict Ultra-Luxury Cinematic Minimalist, pitch black background (#0A0A0A), high-end glassmorphic cards.

- Theme Shifting: Men/Boys (Cyber Gold/Blue), Women/Girls (Rose Gold/Champagne), Kids/Teens (Purple/Neon Green).

Key UI/UX Screens & Features to Build:

1. Dynamic Language Selector: Floating luxury pill (English, العربية, Français, Español, Português, Deutsch).

2. Deep Onboarding with Custom Goals & Smart AI Solutions:

   - Profile Creation: Capture Name, Gender, and AGE.

   - Dynamic Goal Selection Screen: 

     * Preset Luxury Options: (Car, House, Luxury Fashion, Business Fund, High-Income Course).

     * Custom Input Option: An elegant text field titled "Other Target (Write your own goal...)" that allows users to type ANY custom goal, which saves instantly as their visual target.

   - Price & Timeframe Capture: Beautiful inputs for "Total Target Price" and a slider/selector for "Target Timeframe (How many months?)"

   - Interactive AI Solutions Card: Based on the price, months, and income entered, display a simulated dynamic AI insight card:

     * If Realistic: Glow green/gold with a message: "Highly Feasible! Your HOLLI Daily Guardrail will lock this target smoothly in [X] months."

     * If Unrealistic/Too Tight: Display a smart alternative solution card: "HOLLI Smart Suggestion: To protect your peace, extending your timeframe by [X] months reduces your daily stress and updates your Daily Budget to a comfortable [X] amount."

3. The Master Mobile Dashboard & Family Sync:

   - Glowing Ring Chart: Central interactive wheel showing "Remaining Today Budget" matching the active profile's theme color.

   - 🔥 The Streak Counter: Glowing fire icon for "Daily Discipline Streak".

   - 1-Click Fast Expense Logger: FAB button opening a customized quick grid with age/profile specific icons.

   - 🚨 Emergency Brake Button with AI Soft Guard to absorb overspending.

4. 🏆 HOLLI Squad (Family Leaderboard) & Parent Radar Hub:

   - "HOLLI Squad" Leaderboard: Displays family members ranked by streak side-by-side (Father, Mother, Son, Daughter) glowing in their custom profile colors.

   - "Parent Insights Hub": A dedicated layout for Parents to supervise linked children's active discipline streaks and custom goals remotely.

   - "Vision Reward Vault": Displays the custom-typed goal with a gorgeous tracking progress bar funded by daily savings.

5. Premium Subscription Page (Pricing Screen):

   - Ultra-premium screen featuring Monthly Pass ($20/mo) and Annual Legacy Pass ($200/yr with 2 Months FREE). Free access trigger if they invite 20 friends.

Technical UI Specs:

- Tailored perfectly for mobile boundaries (max-w-md mx-auto).

- Complete client-side simulation: Typing a custom goal, adjusting the timeframe slider, or selecting a profile must instantly update the AI Solutions card, the entire visual theme, and calculation metrics in real-time.
